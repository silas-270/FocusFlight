# Third-party notices

Blocktime ships data, models and artwork made by others. The app lists all of them under
**Settings → Credits & licenses** (`data/model/CreditsCatalog.kt`); this file covers the licenses
that also apply to the files in this repository.

## `app/src/main/assets/flights.db`

- **Routes:** derived from [OpenFlights](https://openflights.org/data) route data, licensed under
  the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
- **Airports and runways:** from [OurAirports](https://ourairports.com/data/), public domain.

`flights.db` is a derived database of the OpenFlights data and is made available under the ODbL 1.0.
