# Streaks

**Status: ⚪ shelved** — parked 2026-08-27, revisit after Story/Free/
Challenges ship and real usage patterns exist.

## Why this came up

Streaks (Duolingo-style "don't break the chain") are usually the
single highest-leverage retention mechanic in gamified apps — they
answer "why open the app again tomorrow," which none of the three
modes answer on their own.

## Why it's shelved rather than built

A strict daily streak fits low-commitment activities (Duolingo: 2-3
minutes/day). FocusFlight's minimum session is ~30 minutes. That
mismatch is a known failure pattern: people break the chain on a
genuinely busy day, feel like the whole streak is ruined, and abandon
the app entirely rather than just miss one day.

## If revisited

The likely better shape, if/when this comes back: a **weekly target**
(e.g. "4 sessions this week") with flexibility on which days, rather
than an unbroken daily chain. Not designed further than that — this is
a placeholder for a future conversation, not a spec.
